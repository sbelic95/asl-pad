Inoffizielles Crew-Pad für Air Serbia Virtual – nur für die Flugsimulation.

In short

  • ASL Pad runs entirely in your browser. There is no server and no database of ours behind it.
  • Everything the pad keeps (settings, your flights, your logbook, your vAMSYS sign-in) stays on your own device.
  • We do not collect, sell or share your data. No analytics, no advertising, no tracking cookies.
  • You can delete everything at any time: sign out of vAMSYS in the settings of the pad, or clear the website data of the pad in your browser.

Who runs ASL Pad

ASL Pad is an unofficial fan project for the pilots of Air Serbia Virtual, made by the GitHub user sbelic95. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Air Serbia. The source code is public: github.com/sbelic95/asl-pad.

Questions about privacy: open an issue on GitHub or ask in the Discord of Air Serbia Virtual.

Signing in with vAMSYS

The sign-in only works once Air Serbia Virtual has activated ASL Pad in vAMSYS. Without a sign-in the pad does not connect to vAMSYS at all.

If you choose to sign in with your Air Serbia Virtual account on vAMSYS, you sign in on the vAMSYS website itself – ASL Pad never sees your password. vAMSYS then gives the pad an access token for your account. The pad keeps this token only on your device and uses it to read the following, depending on what you allow:

PermissionWhat the pad does with it
Name and e-mail addressShows your name in the pad. The e-mail address is not shown or used.
Network IDs (VATSIM, IVAO …)Fills in your VATSIM ID so the pad can find your aircraft on the VATSIM network.
Pilot profile, rank, stats, badgesShows your official rank and hours.
Bookings, PIREPs, logbookShows your booked flight and your logbook of the airline.
Activities (events, tours, rosters)Shows the events and tours of the airline; with your permission you can register for them.

The pad only reads and shows this data on your device. It does not send it anywhere else. Signing out deletes the token and the copied data from the device. You can also withdraw the access of ASL Pad at any time in your vAMSYS account.

What is stored on your device

  • Settings: language, units, look, wallpaper (also a photo you choose), home screen cards, your SimBrief user name or ID, your VATSIM ID and your Hoppie logon code.
  • Your flights: times, track and fuel checks of the current flight, and your logbook.
  • Bookings made in the pad, fuel changes on the plan card, and the vAMSYS sign-in (if used).
  • Copies of public data for offline use (airline notices, events, weather, ground maps).
  • What you enter in the apps: calendar entries, alarms and timer, kneeboard notes and drawings, check-ins, flight reports, the preflight list and the notifications of the pad. The PIN is not stored: it is the digits of your vAMSYS pilot ID.

All of this is kept in the browser storage of this website on your device. It never leaves the device unless you export your logbook yourself.

Services the pad talks to

To show live data, your browser fetches it directly from these services. They see your IP address like with any website and work under their own privacy policies:

ServiceWhat for, and what is sent
GitHub Pages / GitHubHosts the pad and its data files (notices, events, weather, ground maps).
vAMSYSOnly if you sign in: your access token, to read the data listed above.
SimBrief (Navigraph)Your SimBrief user name or ID, to load your latest flight plan.
VATSIMPublic network data and weather; nothing personal is sent.
vIFF CDMThe callsign of your flight, to read your slot times.
Hoppie ACARS (hoppie.nl)Only if you enter a logon code: the code and your callsign, to read and send your ACARS and CPDLC messages.
VATSpy data project, SimAware TRACON (GitHub)Airspace boundaries for the frequency guide; nothing personal is sent.
OpenStreetMap (tile.openstreetmap.org), OverpassMap tiles and airport stand data.
pics.avs.ioThe airline logo.
IVAO (api.ivao.aero)Only while the IVAO Radar is open: the public list of flights and stations; nothing personal is sent. Your IVAO VID stays on the device.
aviationweather.gov, metar.vatsim.netWeather (METAR, TAF) for the Air Serbia destinations; only airport codes are sent.
ASL Pad server (Cloudflare)Not switched on yet. If the airline switches it on, your logbook, calendar entries, alarms and check-ins are kept under your vAMSYS pilot ID so that your devices show the same data. This page will be updated before that happens.

Your rights

As all data stays on your device, you are in full control of it: you can view it in the pad, change it in the settings and delete it at any time. For the data held by vAMSYS or Air Serbia Virtual, please contact the airline or vAMSYS directly.

Prijava preko vAMSYS-a

Prijava radi tek kad Air Serbia Virtual aktivira ASL Pad u vAMSYS-u. Bez prijave pad se uopšte ne povezuje sa vAMSYS-om.

Ako se prijaviš svojim nalogom Air Serbia Virtual na vAMSYS-u, prijavljuješ se na samom sajtu vAMSYS-a – ASL Pad nikad ne vidi tvoju lozinku. vAMSYS zatim padu daje pristupni token za tvoj nalog. Pad čuva taj token samo na tvom uređaju i koristi ga da pročita sledeće, u zavisnosti od onoga što dozvoliš:

DozvolaŠta pad radi sa njom
Ime i e-adresaPrikazuje tvoje ime u padu. E-adresa se ne prikazuje i ne koristi.
ID-jevi mreža (VATSIM, IVAO …)Upisuje tvoj VATSIM ID da bi pad našao tvoj avion na mreži.
Profil pilota, čin, statistika, značkePrikazuje tvoj zvanični čin i sate.
Rezervacije, PIREP-ovi, dnevnikPrikazuje tvoj rezervisani let i dnevnik letenja kompanije.
Aktivnosti (događaji, ture, rasporedi)Prikazuje događaje i ture kompanije; uz tvoju dozvolu možeš se prijaviti na njih.

Pad ove podatke samo čita i prikazuje na tvom uređaju i ne šalje ih nigde dalje. Odjava briše token i kopirane podatke sa uređaja. Pristup ASL Pada možeš u svakom trenutku ukinuti i u svom vAMSYS nalogu.

Šta se čuva na tvom uređaju

  • Podešavanja: jezik, jedinice, izgled, pozadina (i fotografija koju izabereš), kartice početnog ekrana, SimBrief korisničko ime ili ID, VATSIM ID i Hoppie logon kod.
  • Tvoji letovi: vremena, putanja i provere goriva tekućeg leta, i dnevnik letenja.
  • Rezervacije napravljene u padu, izmene goriva na kartici plana i prijava na vAMSYS (ako je koristiš).
  • Kopije javnih podataka za rad bez interneta (obaveštenja, događaji, vreme, mape aerodroma).
  • Ono što uneseš u aplikacije: unosi u kalendaru, alarmi i tajmer, beleške i crteži u Kneeboardu, prijave na dužnost, izveštaji o letu, lista pre leta i obaveštenja pada. PIN se ne čuva: to su cifre tvog vAMSYS ID-a pilota.

Sve se čuva u memoriji pretraživača za ovaj sajt na tvom uređaju i ne napušta uređaj, osim ako sam izvezeš dnevnik letenja.

Anmeldung mit vAMSYS

Die Anmeldung funktioniert erst, wenn Air Serbia Virtual ASL Pad in vAMSYS freigeschaltet hat. Ohne Anmeldung verbindet sich das Pad überhaupt nicht mit vAMSYS.

Wenn du dich mit deinem Konto von Air Serbia Virtual bei vAMSYS anmeldest, geschieht das auf der Website von vAMSYS selbst – ASL Pad sieht dein Passwort nie. vAMSYS gibt dem Pad danach einen Zugangstoken für dein Konto. Das Pad speichert ihn nur auf deinem Gerät und liest damit, je nachdem was du erlaubst:

BerechtigungWas das Pad damit macht
Name und E-Mail-AdresseZeigt deinen Namen im Pad. Die E-Mail-Adresse wird weder angezeigt noch verwendet.
Netzwerk-IDs (VATSIM, IVAO …)Trägt deine VATSIM-ID ein, damit das Pad dein Flugzeug im Netzwerk findet.
Pilotenprofil, Rang, Statistik, AbzeichenZeigt deinen offiziellen Rang und deine Stunden.
Buchungen, PIREPs, LogbuchZeigt deinen gebuchten Flug und das Logbuch der Airline.
Aktivitäten (Events, Touren, Dienstpläne)Zeigt Events und Touren der Airline; mit deiner Erlaubnis kannst du dich dafür anmelden.

Das Pad liest und zeigt diese Daten nur auf deinem Gerät und schickt sie nirgendwohin weiter. Abmelden löscht den Token und die kopierten Daten vom Gerät. Den Zugriff von ASL Pad kannst du außerdem jederzeit in deinem vAMSYS-Konto entziehen.

Was auf deinem Gerät gespeichert wird

  • Einstellungen: Sprache, Einheiten, Aussehen, Hintergrund (auch ein eigenes Foto), Karten des Home-Bildschirms, dein SimBrief-Benutzername oder deine ID, deine VATSIM-ID und dein Hoppie-Logon-Code.
  • Deine Flüge: Zeiten, Flugweg und Spritkontrollen des aktuellen Flugs sowie dein Logbuch.
  • Buchungen im Pad, Sprit-Änderungen auf der Plan-Karte und die vAMSYS-Anmeldung (falls genutzt).
  • Kopien öffentlicher Daten für die Nutzung ohne Internet (Mitteilungen, Events, Wetter, Rollkarten).
  • Was du in den Apps einträgst: Kalendereinträge, Wecker und Timer, Notizen und Zeichnungen im Kniebrett, Check-ins, Flugberichte, die Vorflug-Liste und die Mitteilungen des Pads. Die PIN wird nicht gespeichert: Sie besteht aus den Ziffern deiner vAMSYS-Piloten-ID.

All das liegt im Browserspeicher dieser Website auf deinem Gerät und verlässt es nicht, außer du exportierst dein Logbuch selbst.
